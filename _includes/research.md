Research Interests
======

- Global flood risk and river dynamics <br>
- Hydroinformatics and machine learning <br>
- Sediment transport and river geomorphology <br>

Prospective students and collaborators
======
I welcome enquiries from prospective students and researchers interested in river networks, flood hydrology, catchment geomorphology, remote sensing and environmental data science.

I am happy to explore co-supervision, fellowship and scholarship opportunities with strong candidates whose interests align with my research.

I am also open to collaborations involving global river datasets, flood-frequency analysis, hydrogeomorphic modelling and interpretable machine learning.
